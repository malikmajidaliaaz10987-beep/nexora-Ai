# NEXORA AI — All-in-One SaaS Platform

> **"One Platform. Limitless Possibilities."**

NEXORA AI is an international, enterprise-grade, all-in-one AI workspace designed for entrepreneurs, freelancers, creators, and small businesses worldwide.

---

## 🌟 The 7 Core AI Studios

1. **AI Writing Studio:** 18 generative modes covering SEO articles, blog posts, sales emails, cold outreach, viral social carousels, YouTube scripts, client proposals, paraphrasing, and multi-language translation.
2. **AI Image Studio:** High-resolution visual synthesis with aspect ratio controls (1:1, 16:9, 9:16, 4:3), styles (Cinematic, Photorealistic 8K, 3D Isometric), and canvas previews.
3. **AI Video Studio:** Short-form Reels and YouTube Shorts script generation, hook-hold-payoff pacing, scene-by-scene storyboard breakdowns, and render pipeline status.
4. **AI Document Studio:** Interactive Chat with PDF & Documents (interrogate SLAs and contracts), executive summarizer, ATS resume builder, and targeted cover letters.
5. **AI Chatbot Studio:** Build custom chatbots grounded in your business knowledge documents/FAQs. Includes live simulator and an embeddable `<script>` website widget.
6. **AI Marketing Studio:** 30-day launch campaign planner, multi-platform ad copy variants (Google RSA, Facebook/Instagram, LinkedIn), SEO keyword clusters, and brand voice guidelines.
7. **AI Business Assistant:** Validated deterministic line-item invoice & quotation engine paired with AI executive summaries, investor business plans, and meeting notes action items.

---

## 🚀 Key Architectural Features

- **Server-Side AI Layer:** Integrates Google Gemini 3.8 Flash using `@google/genai` exclusively from server endpoints. Secrets never touch the browser.
- **Server-Authoritative Credit System:** Atomic deduction and automatic refund on failed operations.
- **25-Table Relational Schema:** Production-ready PostgreSQL / Supabase migration script located in `/database/001_initial_schema.sql`.
- **Role-Based Access Control:** Visitor, Free, Starter, Pro, Business, and Administrator roles with protected routes and audit trails.
- **Premium Dark Cinematic Design:** Handcrafted palette `#080B14`, `#101625`, `#131C2D`, with neon violet (`#8B5CF6`) and cyan (`#06B6D4`) accents.
- **Live Demo Switcher:** Immediate one-click role switching between Free User, Pro Creator, and Administrator for frictionless evaluation.
